# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is not a substitute for it.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, as described in
[SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md#contacting-the-maintainer). Reports are reviewed
promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour
breaks this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
